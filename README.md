# Cozy Desktop Pet

A small pixel pet that lives on your desktop. Stares at your screen, naps in its house, and keeps you company while you work.

## Status
In development. Art and house design are done; desktop app is in progress.

## Planned features
- Dog or cat, with a name you choose
- Stares while you code, copies your actions, knocks the screen with sound
- Popcorn when you watch videos
- Food, water, bone, toys, and cozy sleep
- Quiet mode and auto-hide in fullscreen and meetings
- Saves your pet between restarts

## Privacy
The pet only reads the active window title and whether you're typing. It never logs what you type or takes screenshots.
